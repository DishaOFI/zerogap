confirm the applicant has been identified without ambiguity no unresolved duplicate or conflicting case 2 Compare the case file against the mandatory order information checklist in Section 3 field by field 3 Where the ZeroGap Agent performs the check review its completeness result and confidence flags before relying on them for a missinginformation decision 4 If every mandatory field is present and consistent mark the case ready for order transmission and proceed to Section 6 of the Deadline Escalation Procedure 5 If any mandatory field is missing unreadable or contradictory eg mismatched applicant name between the application and an identity document mark the case incomplete and proceed to Section 5 6 Record the completeness outcome the fields checked and the reviewer or agent identifier in the case log 5 MissingInformation Handling 7 Prepare a missinginformation request Aufforderung listing precisely which mandatory fields or documents are outstanding 8 Route the request for human review before dispatch the ZeroGap Agent drafts the communication but does not send it unattended 9 Send the request to the applicant or their authorized representative without delay The statutory decision clock is paused from the day this request is received by the applicant not from the day the gap was identified do not delay dispatch once the gap is confirmed 10 Log the dispatch date in the case file this date starts the pause of the applicable decision deadline 11 On receipt of the completed information rerun the completeness check Section 4 before resuming case handling 12 If the applicant does not respond within the locally agreed followup window refer to the Deadline Escalation Procedure for the applicable escalation trigger 6 Roles Case Handler Sachspezialist für Pflege owns the case makes the final completeness determination and approves outgoing correspondence ZeroGap Agent performs the automated completeness check against Section 3 flags missing or inconsistent fields and drafts the missinginformation request for human review Input Output Management scans classifies and triages incoming documents and resubmissions and dispatches approved correspondence Team Lead reviews cases where the ZeroGap Agents confidence flag is low or where the applicant has not responded to a request 7 Escalation Escalate to the Team Lead when the ZeroGap Agent cannot reach a confident completeness determination the applicant disputes a missinginformation request or a repeat request is required for the same case See the Deadline Escalation Procedure for timebased escalation triggers 8 Closure Criteria All mandatory order information fields are present consistent and evidenced in the case file The completeness outcome and revieweragent identifier
