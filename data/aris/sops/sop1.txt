Case Handover Ownership Protocol Controlled transfer of a case between Input Output Management the Case Handler the Medical Service and the Decision Intelligence Agent NordCare Pflegekasse LongTerm Care Claims Operations 1 Purpose This protocol defines how ownership of a longterm care case is transferred in a controlled traceable manner between the functions and agents involved in processing an application from intake through to decision and where applicable objection handling It ensures that at every point in the process exactly one party is accountable for the case 2 Scope Applies to every application for determination or change of longterm care need Pflegebedürftigkeit handled by NordCare Pflegekasse from receipt of the Antrag to case closure Covers handovers between Input Output Management the Case Handler the Medical Service MD the Decision Intelligence Agent and where an objection is raised the objectionhandling function Does not cover the Medical Services own internal assessment workflow which is a thirdparty activity outside NordCares operational control 3 Ownership Model Input Output Management owns the case from receipt of the application until the Case Handler has created the case record and confirmed applicant identification The Case Handler Sachspezialist für Pflege owns the case from case creation through deadlinetype determination confirmation letter dispatch information gathering and completeness checking up to transmission of the assessment order Ownership passes to the Medical Service MD for the duration of the assessment NordCare retains monitoring responsibility but not operational control during this period The Case Handler resumes ownership on return of the assessment report Gutachten and assignment of the Gutachten to the case The Decision Intelligence Agent holds working ownership of the plausibility check and draft benefitentitlement determination under the Case Handlers supervision until the benefit decision is documented The Case Handler owns issuance dispatch and archiving of the care decision notice Pflegebescheid Where an objection Widerspruch is submitted ownership passes to the objectionhandling function on receipt and triage of the objection 4 When a Handover Is Required The case moves from intake Input Output Management to case handling Case Handler The assessment order is transmitted to the Medical Service The Gutachten is returned by the Medical Service and assigned to the case The Decision Intelligence Agents plausibility check and draft decision are handed to the Case Handler for review and finalization The benefit decision is documented and the care decision notice is generated sent and archived closing the case An objection is received and the case is triaged to the objectionhandling function 5 Mandatory Handover Package Case reference and applicant identification Current process stage and status eg awaiting MD assessment Gutachten received decision drafted Actions completed to date with dates and the responsible party for each Open items and any outstanding requests eg a missinginformation request still awaiting a response Applicable due date deadline type and current pauseresume status per the Deadline Escalation Procedure Named sending owner and named receiving owner both explicitly acknowledging the transfer 6 Handover Procedure 1 The sending owner assembles the mandatory handover package described in Section 5 and attaches it to the case record 2 The sending owner confirms the case is in a consistent state no unresolved data conflicts all completed actions logged before initiating the handover 3 The receiving owner reviews the handover package and either accepts ownership or returns the case with a documented reason if the package is incomplete 4 On acceptance the case management system timestamps the change of ownership and records both the sending and receiving owner 5 Where the Decision Intelligence Agent is the receiving party its output remains subject to Case Handler review before any decision is finalized agent output is never transmitted to the applicant unattended 6 The sending owner remains available to answer clarifying questions for a reasonable period after handover particularly where the Medical Service or the applicant requests clarification on information supplied before the handover 7 Ownership During Repeat Assessments and Objections Where a repeat assessment Wiederholungsbegutachtung is triggered the Case Handler who owned the original case retains ownership of the new assessment cycle where practicable to preserve continuity of case knowledge a formal handover under Section 6 still applies if the case is reassigned to a different Case Handler Where an objection is received ownership passes to the objectionhandling function but the original Case Handlers case history the Gutachten and the documented benefit decision must be included in full in the handover package since the objection review depends on this record 8 Closure Criteria The care decision notice has been sent and archived or the objection process if any has reached a final outcome All handovers relevant to the case are logged with sending owner receiving owner and timestamp No open items remain unresolved at the point of closure The complete case record including the Gutachten and the documented benefit decision is retained per NordCares recordsretention rules 9 Roles Input Output Management scans classifies and triages incoming documents owns the case until initial case creation Case Handler Sachspezialist für
