Your Long-Term Care Application: What Happens Next

Dear Mrs. Hoffmann,
Thank you for submitting your application for long-term care benefits (case reference NC-2026-081345). We are glad to support you through this process, and this letter explains what will happen between now and your care-level decision.
The Steps Ahead
The Medical Service (Medizinischer Dienst, MD) will carry out an independent assessment of your care needs.
The MD will contact you directly to arrange a convenient time for this assessment, either at home or, if you are currently hospitalized, at your place of care.
As soon as the assessment is complete, we will inform you of the outcome without delay.
How the Assessment Works
A trained assessor from the Medical Service will visit you to understand exactly what kind of support you need day to day. During this visit, the assessor will look at:
Which care level (Pflegegrad) best reflects your current situation
What kind of support would help stabilize or improve things
How much care and assistance you are likely to need, and in what form
It genuinely helps if the person who supports you day to day is present for this conversation, so please feel free to have a family member or your representative join you.
Please let us know if your situation changes.
If you need to be admitted to hospital while your application is being processed, please tell us as soon as possible so we can coordinate the next steps for your assessment with the Medical Service accordingly.

How Long This Will Take
From the date we received your application, we have 25 working days to reach a decision if you are being cared for at home. This is the standard timeframe that applies to most applications like yours.
If we are unable to meet this deadline for reasons within our control, we will pay you €70 for every week the decision is delayed, until the decision is issued. This does not apply if the delay is caused by something outside our control, such as waiting for information we have requested from you.
We're Here to Help
Our care advisory team is available if you have questions about your entitlements or the process itself. They can talk you through your options, help you understand your personal care plan once it is available, and make sure you know what support is available to you at every stage.
If anything about this letter is unclear, or if your circumstances change in the meantime, please get in touch using the contact details below — quoting your case reference NC-2026-081345 helps us assist you faster.

Kind regards,
