Operational Governance & Control Policy
Management controls for ownership, deadlines, escalations, and continuous improvement across the Zero-Gap process — NordCare Pflegekasse, Long-Term Care Claims Operations
1. Purpose
This policy defines the management controls that govern the end-to-end long-term care application process at NordCare Pflegekasse, covering ownership, deadline compliance, escalation handling, and continuous improvement across the completeness-check, deadline-management, case-handover, and objection-handling procedures.
2. Governance Objectives
Ensure clear, single-point accountability for every case at every process stage.
Maintain compliance with statutory commissioning and decision deadlines.
Detect and escalate at-risk or non-compliant cases before statutory consequences are triggered.
Provide management visibility into process performance through a consistent, agreed KPI set.
Drive continuous improvement by identifying and addressing recurring root causes of delay or rework.

4. Control Ownership
Case Handler (Sachspezialist für Pflege): owns case-level control execution, including completeness checks, deadline monitoring, and handover discipline for assigned cases.
Zero-Gap Agent: executes the automated completeness check and surfaces deadline-risk flags for Case Handler and Team Lead review.
Decision Intelligence Agent: executes the Gutachten plausibility check and drafts the benefit-entitlement determination for Case Handler review.
Team Lead: owns operational escalation handling and day-to-day performance monitoring across a team of cases.
Process Owner: owns the overall control framework, KPI reporting, penalty tracking, and approval of any change to the process or its controls.
5. Issue & Improvement Management
Identify the issue through KPI monitoring, escalation records, penalty triggers, or direct case-handler feedback.
Log the issue with the affected case(s), process stage, and observed impact.
Conduct root-cause analysis, distinguishing one-off case errors from systemic process, data, or agent-performance causes.
Define and assign a corrective action, with an owner and target completion date.
Implement the corrective action and verify its effect against the relevant KPI over the following reporting period.
Record the resolved issue and its root cause in the governance log for trend analysis.
6. Records & Evidence
Case-level logs of completeness checks, deadline clock events, handovers, and objection outcomes.
Monthly KPI reporting pack, including penalty payment tracking.
Escalation records with trigger, action taken, and owner.
Issue and improvement log with root causes and corrective actions.
