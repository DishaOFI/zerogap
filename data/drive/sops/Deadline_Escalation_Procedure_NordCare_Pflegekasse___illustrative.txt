Deadline & Escalation Procedure
Managing the statutory commissioning and decision clocks for long-term care applications — NordCare Pflegekasse, Long-Term Care Claims Operations
1. Purpose
This procedure defines how NordCare Pflegekasse determines, tracks, pauses, and escalates the statutory deadlines that apply to long-term care applications, from application receipt through to the written care-level decision.
2. Scope
Applies to every application for determination or change of long-term care need from the point of receipt to dispatch of the written decision (Bescheid).
Covers deadline-type determination, the 3-working-day Medical Service commissioning window, pause/resume handling, and escalation when a deadline is at risk or missed.
3. Deadline Types (Fristtyp)

Deadline type is determined at intake based on the applicant's situation as stated in the application; it does not depend on whether the order information is complete.
4. Clock Start, Pause, and Resume Rules
Both the 3-working-day commissioning clock and the applicable decision clock (5 / 10 / 25 working days) start on the date the application is received, regardless of completeness.
The insurer must commission the Medical Service within 3 working days of receipt using a secure electronic channel, based on the mandatory order information available at that point (see the Completeness Check & Missing-Information Work Instruction).
If mandatory order information is missing, the decision clock is paused only from the day the applicant receives the missing-information request — not from the day the gap was identified, and not automatically.
If the request is delayed after the gap is confirmed, the days between confirmation and dispatch still count against the decision deadline.
The decision clock resumes on the day the requested information is received back at NordCare Pflegekasse.
Log clock-start, pause-start, pause-end, and clock-resume dates in the case file for every application.
5. Escalation Triggers and Levels
Level
Trigger
Action / owner
Level 1 — Case Handler
Case approaching 70% of the applicable decision deadline with no missing-information request yet issued.
Case Handler reviews and either progresses the case or issues a missing-information request without delay.
Level 2 — Team Lead
Mandatory 3-working-day commissioning window is at risk, or applicant has not responded to a missing-information request within the locally agreed follow-up period.
Team Lead reviews case status, confirms whether a further request or direct outreach is warranted, and reassigns capacity if needed.
Level 3 — Process Owner
Standard 25-working-day (or shortened) decision deadline has been missed, triggering the statutory penalty; or a recurring pattern of missed deadlines is identified.
Process Owner logs the penalty trigger, confirms root cause, and reports the case in the governance review.

Current process step and case handler
Reason for escalation and requested action
Named receiving owner and next review date
7. Penalty and Compliance Consequences
If NordCare Pflegekasse fails to issue its written decision within the applicable deadline, it must pay the applicant €70 for every started week the deadline is exceeded, continuing until the decision is issued. This penalty does not apply where the delay is not attributable to NordCare Pflegekasse, in which case the deadline is paused until the delay is resolved.
Legal basis: § 18c Abs. 5 SGB XI.
8. Roles
Case Handler: monitors deadline status for assigned cases and triggers Level 1 escalation.
Zero-Gap Agent: surfaces cases approaching a deadline threshold and flags whether a missing-information request is outstanding.
Team Lead: owns Level 2 escalations and follow-up on non-responsive applicants.
Process Owner: owns Level 3 escalations, penalty tracking, and periodic governance review.
9. Governance
The Process Owner reviews deadline performance monthly, including cases approaching or exceeding statutory deadlines, penalty payments triggered, and recurring root causes (e.g., systematic delay in issuing missing-information requests).
