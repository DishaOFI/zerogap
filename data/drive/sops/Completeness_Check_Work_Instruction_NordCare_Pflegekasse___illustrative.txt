Completeness Check & Missing-Information Work Instruction
Verifying mandatory order information before commissioning the Medical Service (MD) — NordCare Pflegekasse, Long-Term Care Claims Operations
1. Purpose
This work instruction defines how case handlers and the Zero-Gap completeness-check capability verify that an incoming long-term care application contains the mandatory order information required to commission the Medical Service (Medizinischer Dienst, MD) with the determination of care need. It applies from case creation up to transmission of the assessment order.
2. Scope
Applies to all applications for initial determination or change of long-term care need (Pflegebedürftigkeit) received by NordCare Pflegekasse.
Covers process steps: gather case-specific information, check completeness of mandatory order information, request missing information where required, and generate/transmit the assessment order.
Does not cover the Medical Service's own assessment process, which is a third-party activity outside NordCare's operational control.

4. Completeness Check Procedure
Retrieve the case file created at intake and confirm the applicant has been identified without ambiguity (no unresolved duplicate or conflicting case).
Compare the case file against the mandatory order information checklist in Section 3, field by field.
Where the Zero-Gap Agent performs the check, review its completeness result and confidence flags before relying on them for a missing-information decision.
If every mandatory field is present and consistent, mark the case ready for order transmission and proceed to Section 6 of the Deadline & Escalation Procedure.
If any mandatory field is missing, unreadable, or contradictory (e.g., mismatched applicant name between the application and an identity document), mark the case incomplete and proceed to Section 5.
Record the completeness outcome, the fields checked, and the reviewer or agent identifier in the case log.
5. Missing-Information Handling
Prepare a missing-information request (Aufforderung) listing precisely which mandatory fields or documents are outstanding.
Route the request for human review before dispatch; the Zero-Gap Agent drafts the communication but does not send it unattended.
Send the request to the applicant or their authorized representative without delay. The statutory decision clock is paused from the day this request is received by the applicant, not from the day the gap was identified — do not delay dispatch once the gap is confirmed.
Log the dispatch date in the case file; this date starts the pause of the applicable decision deadline.
On receipt of the completed information, re-run the completeness check (Section 4) before resuming case handling.
If the applicant does not respond within the locally agreed follow-up window, refer to the Deadline & Escalation Procedure for the applicable escalation trigger.
6. Roles
Case Handler (Sachspezialist für Pflege): owns the case, makes the final completeness determination, and approves outgoing correspondence.
Zero-Gap Agent: performs the automated completeness check against Section 3, flags missing or inconsistent fields, and drafts the missing-information request for human review.
Input & Output Management: scans, classifies, and triages incoming documents and resubmissions, and dispatches approved correspondence.
Team Lead: reviews cases where the Zero-Gap Agent's confidence flag is low or where the applicant has not responded to a request.
7. Escalation
Escalate to the Team Lead when: the Zero-Gap Agent cannot reach a confident completeness determination; the applicant disputes a missing-information request; or a repeat request is required for the same case. See the Deadline & Escalation Procedure for time-based escalation triggers.
8. Closure Criteria
All mandatory order information fields are present, consistent, and evidenced in the case file.
The completeness outcome and reviewer/agent identifier are logged.
Where a missing-information request was issued, the pause start and resume dates are recorded.
The case is handed to the assessment-order generation step only after these criteria are met.
9. Legal & Regulatory References
§ 18 Abs. 1 SGB XI — insurer must commission the Medical Service within 3 working days of application receipt; MD-Bund defines the mandatory document set for commissioning.
§ 18 Abs. 4 SGB XI — obligation to provide the Medical Service with documents required for the assessment itself (separate from the commissioning data set above).
§ 18c Abs. 6 SGB XI — the decision deadline is paused from the day the applicant receives a request for still-missing mandatory documents, and resumes on receipt.
MD-Bund Begutachtungs-Richtlinien, Ziff. 3.1 and Anlage 1 — defines the Antragsinformationen / Stammdaten mandatory for commissioning.
